Swipe Gallery is designed to help you review, sort, favorite, and delete photos from your device's photo library. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

This policy is a practical privacy notice for the current version of the app. It is not legal advice. If you publish Swipe Gallery, have the final policy reviewed for the laws and app store rules that apply to you.

## Summary

Swipe Gallery processes your photo library on your device. We do not operate a server for this app, create user accounts, collect analytics, show ads, track you, sell personal information, or upload your photos.

## Information the App Accesses

Swipe Gallery asks for access to your device's media library so it can provide its core features. Depending on your device and operating system, the permission prompt may refer to photos, media, or selected media.

The app may access:

- Photos you allow the app to view
- Photo metadata provided by the operating system, such as asset ID, local URI, creation time, dimensions, file size, and favorite status
- Your in-app sorting decisions, such as whether you marked a photo as "keep" or "remove"
- Deletion history created inside the app, such as deletion date, number of photos deleted, photo IDs, and estimated storage freed

The current app is built for photo organization. It does not intentionally read your contacts, precise location, calendar, messages, microphone, camera, or financial information.

## How the App Uses This Information

Swipe Gallery uses the information above to:

- Display photos for swiping and reviewing
- Group photos by day or month
- Remember which photos you have sorted
- Show progress and storage-saving statistics
- Let you mark photos as favorites where the operating system supports it
- Delete selected photos after you confirm the action

## On-Device Storage

Swipe Gallery stores sorting decisions and deletion history in a local database on your device. This local data helps the app remember your progress and show statistics.

This local database is not uploaded by Swipe Gallery. It remains on your device unless you delete the app, clear the app's data, or the operating system removes the app data.

## Photos and Deletion

Swipe Gallery displays photos from your device's photo library. Your photos are not copied to a Swipe Gallery server.

If you choose to delete photos through the app, Swipe Gallery asks the operating system to delete those selected photo assets. Depending on your device and photo library settings, deleted photos may be moved to a system "Recently Deleted" area before permanent deletion. You should review your device's Photos or Gallery app for recovery and permanent deletion options.

## Data Sharing

Swipe Gallery does not share your photos, photo metadata, sorting decisions, deletion history, or usage activity with us or with third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

The app is distributed through app stores and uses platform services from Apple, Google, Expo, and React Native to run on your device. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies when you download, install, update, or use platform-level services. Swipe Gallery does not receive advertising profiles or tracking data from those providers.

## Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

Swipe Gallery does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or data brokers.

Swipe Gallery does not track you across apps or websites.

## Permissions and Your Choices

You can control photo library access in your device settings.

On iOS, you may be able to grant access to all photos or only selected photos. If you grant limited access, Swipe Gallery can only work with the photos the system makes available to the app.

On Android, media permissions vary by Android version. You can change or revoke permissions in system settings. If photo access is denied or revoked, the app's core photo organization features may not work.

You can remove local Swipe Gallery data by deleting the app or clearing the app's storage through your device settings.

## Children

Swipe Gallery is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Swipe Gallery keeps its app data on your device and relies on your device's operating system protections. You can help protect your photos and app data by using a device passcode, biometric lock, and up-to-date operating system software.

No method of storage or processing is perfectly secure, but Swipe Gallery minimizes risk by not uploading your photos or app activity to a server.

We may update this Privacy Policy if Swipe Gallery's features or data practices change. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
